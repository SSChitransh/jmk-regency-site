GOOD TO KNOW

Before you
check in.

Have a question? Get in touch and the team can clarify the details for your dates.

What time are check-in and check-out? +

The hotel's published reception signage lists check-in at 1:00 PM and check-out at 11:00 AM. Please reconfirm when booking, especially if you need an early check-in or late check-out.

Is breakfast included? +

Breakfast is offered with applicable room plans. Confirm the meal plan and inclusions for your chosen dates before paying.

Can the hotel arrange airport or railway transfers? +

Transfer assistance is listed among the hotel's services. Availability, pickup details and charges should be confirmed directly with the hotel.

How do I confirm a room and final price? +

Send an enquiry with your dates and room preference using the form above, or call the hotel. Your stay is not confirmed until the property responds with availability and booking terms.
